Design principle
The host is the murder victim inside the fiction, but continues operating the app outside the fiction. Every "message from Aditya" must have been recorded before the fictional death. Later police and forensic findings are delivered by the case system, not by an omniscient ghost. The host controls pacing and never competes as a suspect.
