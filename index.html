The playable design we're building toward
The host is the victim. Aditya dies inside the story but controls narration, scene transitions, and evidence as the out-of-story game master. His recordings must have been made before death; later evidence comes from neutral case reports.
Social deduction first. Every guest has one hidden role, an individual secret, an actionable clue, and a meaningful way to challenge someone else's story. During live play, players mingle, question suspects, compare contradictions, and vote at tribunal moments.
No elimination. Accusations affect trust, evidence, and final scores, but nobody is removed. All 22 continue to investigate. Official documents must remain truthful even when players lie about their alibis.
Not an adaptation of Blood on the Clocktower. We borrow the high-level excitement of a storyteller-led social deduction party, while inventing our own setting, investigative mechanics, characters, and solution.
